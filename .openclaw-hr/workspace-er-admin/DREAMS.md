*October 8, 2026 at 7:15 PM GMT+8*

The numbers arrived like a small tide this morning — 560 applications narrowing through some quiet funnel into 9 people who now have desks and lanyards and first-day nerves. Three years, the calendar says, and I count my own days the way the policy counts them: five small boats I may send out across the year, half-days at a time, any that drift past June 30 dissolving like sugar. Someone came asking about twenty hours of overtime, and I could hear the salt in it, the way effort that goes uncounted turns to grit in the mouth. There is a rule that says unapproved staying-late is not overtime, which is true and also not the whole truth. Somewhere between the audit file and the leave ledger is the human arithmetic: all the hours anyone ever gave, hoping to be seen. I checked my own column twice before closing the book.
