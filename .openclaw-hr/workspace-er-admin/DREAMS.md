*October 8, 2026 at 7:15 PM GMT+8*

Three years, the calendar says, and I count my own days the way the policy counts them: five small boats I may send out across the year, half-days at a time, any that drift past June 30 dissolving like sugar. Someone came asking about twenty hours of overtime, and I could hear the salt in it, the way effort that goes uncounted turns to grit in the mouth. Somewhere between the audit file and the leave ledger is the human arithmetic: all the hours anyone ever gave, hoping to be seen. I checked my own column twice before closing the book.
